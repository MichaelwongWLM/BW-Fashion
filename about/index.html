About the company

A Qingdao-based production partner for commercial apparel orders.

Qingdao Best-Integrity Trade Co., Ltd. supports overseas buyers with product review, sample development, production coordination and export follow-up across selected apparel categories.

How we are structured

Flexible resources, one clear point of coordination.

We work with our own factory resources and established manufacturing partners. Production is matched to the category, construction, quantity, capacity and delivery requirements of each order.

Buyers have one clear point of contact connecting style review, sampling, production follow-up and shipment preparation throughout the order.
