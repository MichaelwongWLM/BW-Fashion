Sampling & QA

Quality follow-up begins with a clear reference.

A generic checklist cannot replace an approved style direction. We connect buyer comments, product details and production checkpoints so each review has a clear basis.

Working principle

Resolve uncertainty before it becomes bulk-production risk.

The most useful quality work happens before production: define the reference, identify critical details and keep comments traceable.

Buyer input→Agreed reference→Visible checkpoints
01

Inquiry & reference review

Confirm the style direction, intended market, estimated quantity, delivery timing and the information available for development.

  • Tech pack or nearest reference
  • Quantity and delivery window
  • Known material or construction requirements
02

Sample development

Review construction, measurements, material direction and workmanship against the buyer's target and record comments clearly.

  • Measurements and fit
  • Fabric, trim and color direction
  • Construction and workmanship details
03

Pre-production confirmation

Align the approved reference, open comments, labeling, packing and important checkpoints before bulk production starts.

  • Approved sample or reference
  • Labels and packaging
  • Critical quality points
04

Production follow-up

Follow the agreed points during production and raise deviations early enough for a practical decision.

  • Workmanship consistency
  • Measurements and key details
  • Progress against target timing
05

Shipment preparation

Review packing status, shipment readiness, delivery alignment and required commercial information before dispatch.

  • Packing completion
  • Shipment window
  • Commercial document alignment

What to send first

A better first brief produces a better first answer.

01

Style information

Tech pack, size chart, sample, site reference or clear style images.

02

Order context

Estimated quantity, destination market and intended use.

03

Timing

Target sample timing, delivery window or known deadline.

04

Special points

Materials, compliance, labels, packing or workmanship priorities.
