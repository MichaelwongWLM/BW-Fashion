Capabilities

Production support built around the actual style.

Our role is to connect product requirements, production resources and export follow-up in one clear working sequence. The scope is agreed style by style, so the proposed route stays relevant to the actual order.

01

Style feasibility review

We review the construction, reference material, intended market, estimated quantity and delivery window before proposing a development route.

  • Tech pack, size chart, reference sample or style photo
  • Construction and workmanship considerations
  • Likely MOQ range and sample timing
02

Sample development

Sampling is managed around the buyer's priorities, with comments converted into specific revision points and an agreed reference for the next stage.

  • Material and trim direction
  • Measurements and fit comments
  • Construction details and workmanship
03

Production planning

Orders are matched to suitable production resources according to category, construction, capacity, quantity and delivery requirements.

  • Pre-production reference alignment
  • Labeling and packing requirements
  • Key production checkpoints
04

Quality follow-up

Follow-up focuses on the points agreed with the buyer rather than a generic checklist detached from the style.

  • Approved sample and comment tracking
  • Workmanship and measurement checks
  • Packing and shipment-readiness review
05

Commercial coordination

Quotation, delivery and shipping discussions are kept connected to the confirmed product specifications and order structure.

  • MOQ and quantity discussion
  • FOB Qingdao and other agreed terms
  • Delivery window and document alignment
06

Selected test orders

For a new relationship, one suitable non-urgent style can be a practical way to evaluate communication, sampling and execution.

  • Low-commitment feasibility review
  • Clear scope before development
  • A realistic basis for future decisions

Production model

The right resource for the right order.

Production is arranged through our own factory resources and established manufacturing partners. The route depends on the garment category, construction, quantity, capacity and required timing.

This structure is useful for buyers who need a practical additional production option for selected styles, test orders or small and mid-size commercial quantities.
