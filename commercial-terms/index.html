Commercial terms

General information before a specific quotation.

The points below explain our usual discussion basis. Final conditions are confirmed for the actual style, quantity, destination and order requirements.

01

Quotation basis

Pricing is reviewed against the confirmed style, materials, construction, quantity, labeling, packing and delivery requirements.

02

Minimum order quantity

MOQ depends on the product, fabric and trim availability, color breakdown and production route. It is discussed style by style.

03

Shipping basis

FOB Qingdao is a common starting point. Other mutually agreed Incoterms can be reviewed according to the destination and order structure.

04

Samples

Sample type, cost, timing and courier arrangements are confirmed before development begins.

05

Production timing

A delivery window is confirmed after product details, quantity, material availability and required approvals are clear.

06

Payment

Payment terms are agreed in the quotation or sales contract and may vary according to the order and business relationship.

07

Final agreement

Only the final quotation, proforma invoice, sales contract and confirmed specifications govern a specific order.
